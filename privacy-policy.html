TastyBook Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

TastyBook is provided by Team Lalli61. TastyBook is designed to store recipes and app settings on your device.

Information the app uses

The app may use recipe text, images, settings and purchase status that you choose to enter or save. Camera and photo-library access are used only when you choose to scan or import a recipe. Microphone access is used only when you choose Smart Cook Mode voice commands.

Sharing and online services

TastyBook does not sell your personal information. When you choose to scan a recipe, the selected image or text may be sent to the app's recipe-scanning service to provide that feature. When you choose to share a recipe, the app uses the sharing service you select.

Storage and security

Recipes and settings are stored locally on your device unless you choose an online feature. Reasonable technical measures are used, but no electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children

TastyBook is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email lalli61apps@hotmail.com.
